## The project

Audrey Technologies was developing a tablet in the VLSI domain. My part was the Wi-Fi module: I designed it and implemented it on a Xilinx Spartan-6 FPGA.

## How it was built

- **Design:** written in Verilog.
- **Tools:** Xilinx ISE for synthesis, place and route, and programming the FPGA.
- **Testing:** simulation first, then testing and debugging on the board.
